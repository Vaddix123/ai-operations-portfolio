# Sanitized project evidence

These briefs document what was inspected, what Branden Opauski directed and verified, and what remains unproven. They are designed for recruiter and hiring-manager review without publishing credentials, private logs, employer information, personal data, model configuration, or complete source.

## Evidence standard

- **Verified locally:** corroborated by implementation, automated test output, build artifacts, screenshots, or consistent operational documentation.
- **Development-stage:** implemented or tested, but not established through paying customers, production scale, or measured commercial outcomes.
- **Planned:** intentionally excluded from portfolio claims until implementation evidence exists.
