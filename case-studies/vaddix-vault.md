# Vaddix Vault

## Problem

Collection software that uses camera, barcode, OCR, and voice input must preserve user control when identity evidence is incomplete or commands are ambiguous.

## Branden's role

Product owner and technical director for product boundaries, recognition policy, privacy rules, architecture, review behavior, and acceptance expectations. AI tools assisted implementation; the portfolio claims requirements, technical review, testing, and product decisions rather than sole hand-coding.

## Directed and verified

- On-device OCR and barcode recognition with catalog matching.
- Offline voice routing, binder and single-item flows, persistence, and pricing workflows.
- Review queue, manual teaching path, and compact local fingerprints.
- Scan-frame discard, supporting-evidence requirements for automatic registration, and rejection of uncertain identities or low-confidence destructive voice commands.
