## Branden's role

Product owner and technical director for requirements, risk boundaries, acceptance criteria, release gates, and final promotion decisions. AI tools assisted implementation; the portfolio claims direction, review, testing, and release authority rather than sole hand-authorship.

- Capability broker, memory, research, automation, and desktop/mobile surfaces.
- Claude and Codex escalation paths with secret redaction.
