# Vongola Director

## Problem

An AI-operated service business needs more than task automation: the owner must be able to see pending work, review evidence, constrain agent authority, approve consequential actions, and stop unsafe execution.

## Branden's role

Founder and product owner for the operating model, workflow requirements, agent boundaries, acceptance review, and go/no-go decisions. AI tools assisted implementation; the portfolio claims product direction and verification rather than a managed engineering team or sole coding.

## Directed and verified

- Intake, pipeline, proposals, projects, invoices, support, files, and backup workflows.
- Scheduled agent work, owner decisions, escalation paths, and machine-readable delivery and retention rules.
- Human approval for consequential external actions, scoped tools, credential boundaries, local-only execution, and restrictions on financial and unsupervised outbound actions.

## Evidence snapshot

- 223 passing tests.
- 22 screens and 715 controls exercised in the reviewed acceptance run.
- Implemented owner console and workflow artifacts reviewed locally.

## Current boundary

Development-stage, pre-launch, and pre-revenue. One acceptance issue remained open at review time. No clients, adoption, ROI, or production-scale operation is claimed.
