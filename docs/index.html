Operating method

Domain judgment first. Technology in service of the decision.

  1. 01

    Judge the risk

    Start with the failure mode, the decision owner, and what must never happen silently.

  2. 02

    Translate the work

    Turn operating needs into explicit requirements, states, permissions, and acceptance criteria.

  3. 03

    Build the boundary

    Add human approvals, least-capability access, audit evidence, and clear unavailable states.

  4. 04

    Test the decision

    Use automated suites, adversarial review, and release gates to decide what is ready.
